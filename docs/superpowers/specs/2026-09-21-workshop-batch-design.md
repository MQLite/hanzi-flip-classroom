User replaces one-target questions with a shared tray prepared by splitting a preselected batch of reference words. Default **4 words** explicitly confirmed; offer 2/3/4. Pick distinct valid reference words randomly from the current curriculum/grade scope. Use the summed character occurrences across selected words, shuffled into stable slots. No additional distractors: unused characters from the other words supply alternatives. Preserve the modeled wooden desk and glyph blocks.

Select 2–8 characters in order. Exact unfinished batch reference match: collect a normal wood word stamp, remove exactly those physical tiles, leave all other tiles in their original slots, increment reference progress and award one star. Do not advance to a different character. Finish when all batch references are collected.

Unmatched word: keep selected tiles in the center and display teacher decision buttons. No score, consumption, or failure animation before judgment. Acceptance collects a teal wood stamp labeled 教师认可 and restores the original tiles to their slots; rejection animates those tiles returning to their slots. Both preserve reference progress. A previously collected word cannot score or collect twice. Teacher decision uses the submitting team even if another team is selected while pending. Pending decisions survive mode switching and cannot be overwritten by another selection; cancel returns tiles. New round intentionally clears the batch under the existing abandonment confirmation.
